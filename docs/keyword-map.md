# Keyword Strategy & Search Map — Mini Highland Cows

**Source:** real SEMrush AU exports (`Keyword Export/*.csv`, pulled 2026-09-04 and 2026-09-25), not estimates. 15 raw export files, 89,675 unique keyword rows after merging. Filtered down to 8,350 keywords that are actually relevant to this business (see "How this was filtered" below) — the rest was noise: broad-match SEMrush "related keyword" sprawl (dehorning-industry terms for feedlots, off-breed cattle like Brahman/Angus/Hereford/Wagyu, malformed duplicate-word artifacts like "cattle cattle", non-AU geography, brand/navigational noise). Full filtered dataset lives in `Keyword Export/_final.csv` for reference — this file is the curated, page-ready subset.

## How this was filtered
1. **Merged** all 15 CSV exports, de-duplicated by keyword (kept the highest-relevance record where a keyword appeared in more than one export).
2. **Tier A** (7,521 kw) — contains a direct brand/product term: mini/miniature/micro + highland/cow/cattle, teacup, fluffy cow, highland calf/heifer/steer/bull, chondro. Kept regardless of volume — long-tail buyer-intent phrases matter even at low volume.
3. **Tier B** (829 kw) — generic cattle/cow/calf/paddock/halter/polled/dehorn terms, kept only at relevance ≥ 55 and volume ≥ 20, to admit genuinely useful adjacent topics (dehorning, grooming, land requirements) without the feedlot/dairy/beef-industry noise those broad exports were full of.
4. Excluded: off-breed terms (Brahman, Angus, Hereford, Wagyu, Jersey, Holstein, Dexter, Simmental, etc. — different breeds, not this business), pure Navigational intent (brand-search noise), gibberish duplicate-word artifacts, non-AU/off-topic geography.

## ⚠️ Strategic flag — "Farm Experience" cluster conflicts with current biosecurity policy

There is real, low-competition search demand (480/mo at KD 4 for "highland cow experience" alone, plus a full cluster of petting/tour/visit variants) for **on-farm visit content** — but `CLAUDE.md` states MHC operates a strict closed-gate, zero-farm-inspection biosecurity policy: *"We do not conduct on-farm inspections."* Writing content that ranks for "highland cow experience" style queries without offering that experience would either (a) mislead searchers into expecting a farm visit, or (b) rank for the query and then disappoint/bounce, which is bad for both users and rankings long-term.

**This needs your decision before I touch it.** Three honest options:
1. **Skip this cluster entirely** — leave it un-targeted, since it doesn't match the business model.
2. **Address it head-on in content** — a blog post like "Can You Visit Our Highland Cattle? Our Biosecurity Policy Explained" that captures the search intent, explains *why* no farm visits happen, and redirects to what MHC *does* offer (ultra-HD photos, video call viewings, delivery). This can genuinely rank for informational variants of the query without promising something MHC doesn't do.
3. **Reconsider the policy** for a limited, biosecurity-safe visit format (e.g. a separate quarantined visitor area, not the breeding paddocks) — a business decision, not something I should decide for you.

I've left this cluster out of the 20 blog topics below pending your answer.
