## Two photos held back — need a decision, not just facts

Two of the 19 originally-unused photos are **not** in the new listings above, because
they don't look like standard Highland cattle:

- **Fiona.jfif** — short coat, black muzzle, rounded ears. Doesn't match Highland
  conformation.
- **Tara.jfif** — black with a white belt around the middle. That's the Belted Galloway
  colour pattern, not standard Highland (a genuine "Highland Belty" cross exists in some
  studs, but I won't assume that's what this is).

If these are real animals in your herd — a cross, a different breed you also sell, or
I've simply misjudged the photo — tell me what they actually are and I'll add them
properly. Otherwise they stay excluded rather than mislabelled.
